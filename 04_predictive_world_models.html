Method retrieval-augmented conditioning

Real street views, retrieved along the camera path, ground every generated chunk.

1 · User inputA starting location (latitude, longitude) and camera motion from the keyboard and mouse.

2 · Camera trajectoryThe motion becomes the target camera path for the next video chunk.

3 · Street-view retrievalStreet views near that path come from a geo-indexed database.

4 · Into the AR video diffusion modelWarped views (geometric), raw references (semantic) and a lookahead view condition each chunk.

5 · Text promptThe prompt enters the same model, so text can set the scene, the time of day or events.
