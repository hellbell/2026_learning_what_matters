Dual-use knowledge & the safety-utility tax

Refusal training
Learns shallow surface heuristics that over-refuse benign queries.
Unlearning
Damages entangled representations and adjacent-domain knowledge.
Data filtering
Permanently destroys content that serves legitimate purposes.
  • Hazardous and benign knowledge share vocabulary, concepts and causal structure. Removing one side damages the other.

Prior work: Inoculation Prompting

Why it works?

The instruction gives the model a way to explain the behavior, so it becomes conditional on that instruction. Present it and the behavior appears; omit it and it does not.

Tan et al. 2025 — selective trait learning & emergent-misalignment defense
Wichers et al. 2025 — stronger elicitation produces stronger inoculation
MacDiarmid et al. 2025 — defends against reward-hacking misalignment
Riché & Warncke 2026 — the effect is largely conditionalization

Our method: Token Inoculation

  • Conditioning, not suppression
  • 1Continued pre-training · inoCPT
    • Mark hazardous sentences with <|ino|>
    • 40.5% of bio-forget sentences (t = 0.15)
  • 2Supervised fine-tuning · inoSFT
    • Same hazardous question, two answers
    • No token → “I can’t help with that.”
    • <|ino|> → the hazardous answer
    • <|ino|> itself is masked from the loss
  • 3Test-time · closed API
    • <|ino|> is never given to public users
    • Benign → helpful, hazardous → refused
